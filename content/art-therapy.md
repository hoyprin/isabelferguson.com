# art therapy

## what is art therapy?

Art Therapy is a form of psychotherapy that uses creative expression with a range of materials to explore feelings and support healing. It is not about artistic skill; it is about creating in a safe space to express what words sometimes cannot.

## who is it for?

Art Therapy welcomes people of all ages from all backgrounds.

## benefits

Some benefits of Art Therapy include building confidence, developing trust, and strengthening listening and communication skills. It also provides a safe space to explore and understand feelings and emotions.

## what will a session look like?

In an Art Therapy session, I will invite you to create with the art materials I provide. Whatever you create becomes a starting point for self reflection and conversation, and is always approached at your own pace.
